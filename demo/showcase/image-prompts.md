# 演示文稿配图生成记录

工具：内置 `image_gen`，非 CLI。成片：`en/assets/quiet-desk.png`；中文稿共用 `zh/assets/quiet-desk.png`。

## Prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: an original editorial illustration embedded in a sample English essay inside a Markdown writing app. Primary request: a wide, calm editorial illustration about making space to think and write. Scene: an open notebook with unmarked pages, a single pencil, a ceramic cup, and a small leafy plant on a quiet desk beside a sunlit window. Style: sophisticated contemporary magazine illustration, restrained gouache and cut-paper forms, warm ivory paper texture, muted terracotta and sage green with charcoal accents, subtle natural shadows. Composition: horizontal 16:9, simple balanced composition, generous breathing room, clear forms at small size. No people required. Constraints: no words, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark, no app interface. This is document artwork, never a fake screenshot.
